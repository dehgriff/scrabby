Scrabby common-word selection

Generated 26 November 2026 using wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer.
Source: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
Frequency data: https://pypi.org/project/wordfreq/3.1.1/

Selection and modifications:
- English Zipf frequency >= 2.0 (approximately once per 10 million words).
- ASCII words of 4 to 12 letters, intersected with Scrabby's bundled SOWPODS.
- Uppercased, deduplicated and sorted; no frequency scores included.
- 51,966 entries at generation time.

needed to run or deploy the app.
