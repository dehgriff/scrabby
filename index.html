i A quick note on the rules +

Words read left to right and top to bottom. Every uninterrupted row or column of two or more letters must be allowed. Words of four or more letters use SOWPODS; two- and three-letter words use their respective editable lists, which can include custom words outside SOWPODS. All used tiles must connect edge to edge.

Your score = longest word length + number of words + 1 if all 12 tiles are used. A 17-point arrangement is the theoretical maximum. If your letters can’t make it, Scrabby searches for the highest possible score, including arrangements with unused tiles.

Use Reject + use common words to filter words of four or more letters by frequency, keeping your short-word lists. Each new rack defaults to SOWPODS. The solver checks every connected shape in score order. Difficult racks may take longer; you can stop and keep the best result found. Up to four of your 12 tiles can be blanks, each representing any letter. They count towards word length and the all-tiles bonus. Tiles marked ? in the solution show their chosen letters. The bundled list is the traditional 267,751-word SOWPODS list, rather than the latest Collins edition.
